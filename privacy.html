Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

In short: this website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or sign-up forms. We only receive personal information when you choose to call or email us, and we use it only to respond to you.

1. Who we are

This website, i4.institute, is operated by the International Institute of Innovation & Intelligence (“I⁴”, “we”, “us”), a venture of Neurovia AI Innovation Research Pvt. Ltd., headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

This policy explains what personal information we handle when you visit the website or get in touch with us, and the choices you have.

2. Information we receive

Information you give us. If you call us or send us an email, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, organisation, the programme or topic you are asking about, and the content of your message.

Technical information when you browse. When your browser loads the website, standard technical data is exchanged, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the pages and files requested. We do not run our own analytics or tracking tools, so we do not collect or store this data ourselves. It may, however, be recorded in server logs by the services that host and deliver the website (see section 5).

What we do not collect. The website has no account creation, contact forms, newsletter sign-ups or payment features, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information through it.

3. Cookies and tracking

The website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising, social media pixels or other tracking technologies. If this changes in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

4. How we use your information

We use the information you send us only to:

  • respond to your enquiries and provide information about our programmes, partnerships and activities;
  • arrange follow-up conversations that you have asked for;
  • keep reasonable records of our communications with you; and
  • comply with applicable law and protect our legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

5. Third-party services

  • Website hosting (GitHub Pages). The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses and request details as part of delivering the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). The website loads its fonts from Google. When a page loads, your browser contacts Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and browser information. See Google’s privacy policy for details.
  • Communication tools. If you contact us by phone, email or messaging, your information is also handled by the telephone, email or messaging providers you and we use.

These providers handle data under their own privacy policies. The website may also link to other websites; we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

6. Sharing your information

We share personal information only when necessary: with Neurovia AI Innovation Research Pvt. Ltd. and our personnel who need it to respond to you; with service providers who support our communications and operations under appropriate confidentiality obligations; and with courts, regulators or authorities when the law requires it. We do not disclose your information to third parties for their own marketing.

7. Where your information is processed

Some of the services described above may process data on servers outside India. By contacting us or using the website you understand that your information may be transferred and processed in other countries, subject to applicable law.

8. How long we keep information

We keep correspondence only for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, maintain our records, and meet legal or regulatory requirements. After that, we delete it or anonymise it.

9. Security

The website is served over HTTPS. We take reasonable organisational and technical steps to protect the information we hold, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and how it is used;
  • ask us to correct or update inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • ask us to erase your information when it is no longer needed;
  • withdraw any consent you have given, with effect going forward; and
  • raise a grievance with us, and if it is not resolved, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 12. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

11. Children

The website is intended for adults and organisations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 years of age. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
