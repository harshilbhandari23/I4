About us

INTERNATIONAL INSTITUTE OF INNOVATION & INTELLIGENCE

The International Institute of Innovation & Intelligence — I⁴ (I Power 4) — is a next-generation institution where technology, education, research, industry, entrepreneurship, and innovation converge. A venture of Neurovia AI Innovation Research Pvt. Ltd., headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, I⁴ unites advanced laboratories, industry-aligned programmes, and applied research across artificial intelligence, data science, robotics, and emerging technologies. At I⁴, students, professionals, and leaders learn by doing, transforming ideas into prototypes and prototypes into enterprises. Anchored in strategic industry partnerships and a vibrant innovation ecosystem, I⁴ develops future-ready talent and visionary entrepreneurs who will shape tomorrow's industries and advance sustainable innovation worldwide.

purpose & direction

Our Mission & Vission

Empowering the next generation of innovators and driving national transformation through technology and intelligence.

Our Mission

Building Future Capabilities & Real-World Impact

Our mission is to empower learners, professionals, and leaders to thrive in a rapidly evolving technological world. Through industry-focused education, hands-on research, and advanced laboratories, we transform ideas into successful ventures, nurture entrepreneurs, and develop future-ready talent, thereby shaping the industries of tomorrow and advancing sustainable innovation in India and around the world.

Our Vision

Preparing India to Create, Lead & Innovate

To be a globally recognized center of excellence for laboratory-based learning, skills development, and applied research, where transformative ideas flourish and scientific breakthroughs become solutions of lasting value. We envision a thriving innovation ecosystem that empowers entrepreneurs and technology leaders, strengthens India's innovation leadership, and shapes a sustainable future at local, national, and global levels.
